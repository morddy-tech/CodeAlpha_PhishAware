Frequently asked questions

Is PhishAware a real phishing tool?

No. It is strictly defensive and educational. All scenarios use fictional organizations and domains (example.com and friends). No real phishing campaigns are ever conducted.

Do the fake login pages collect my password?

Never. The simulated login fields are decorative. Whatever you type is discarded instantly and never stored, logged, or transmitted anywhere.

What data does PhishAware store about me?

Only your account details, your training progress (modules, lessons, quizzes, simulations), achievements, and any feedback you choose to submit. No passwords beyond your account password (stored hashed), no banking data, no MFA codes.

How is the Security Awareness Score calculated?

It is a weighted average: training progress (40%), quiz average (35%), and simulation performance (25%).

Do I need to create an account?

You can browse training content and view simulations as a guest, but you need an account to track progress, take quizzes, earn achievements, and submit answers.

Can I retake quizzes?

Yes. Your previous attempts are recorded, and you can retry any quiz as many times as you like.
