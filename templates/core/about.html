About PhishAware

{{ site_name }} is a defensive cybersecurity awareness training platform built as Task 2 (Phishing Awareness Training) of the CodeAlpha Cyber Security internship.

Our mission

Phishing remains the #1 way attackers breach organizations because it targets human trust rather than technical flaws. The most effective defense is a well-trained, skeptical workforce. PhishAware exists to deliver that training through realistic — but completely safe — practice.

What we teach

  • Recognizing phishing emails, fake websites, SMS (smishing) and calls (vishing)
  • Social engineering tactics and Business Email Compromise
  • Safe password and MFA habits
  • Exactly how to respond when you suspect — or click — a threat

Ethics and safety

Every simulation in PhishAware is defensive and educational. We use fictional organizations and domains (like example.com). No real credentials are ever collected, stored, or transmitted. The fake login pages are decorative — any text typed into them is discarded instantly. PhishAware cannot be used to conduct real phishing.

Built for CodeAlpha

This project fulfills the CodeAlpha Cyber Security Internship Task 2 brief: phishing awareness training covering education, fake website recognition, social engineering, best practices, real-world examples, and interactive quizzes.
