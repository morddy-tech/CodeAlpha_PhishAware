Privacy statement

Last updated: {% now "F Y" %}

What we store

  • Your account: username, email, first/last name, and a securely hashed password.
  • Optional profile: avatar, organization, job title, timezone, theme preference.
  • Your learning activity: module and lesson completions, quiz and simulation results, achievements, and notifications.
  • Any feedback you voluntarily submit.

What we never store

  • Passwords typed into simulation login fields (they are discarded instantly).
  • Banking information, credit card data, or MFA codes.
  • Your real passwords for third-party services.
  • Unnecessary device or location information.

Data use

We only use your data to deliver training and track your progress. We do not sell data, and we do not share learning results with third parties. If you submit feedback, it is visible to platform administrators only.

Your choices

You can update your profile and notification preferences at any time from the Profile page. To delete your account, contact the platform administrator.
