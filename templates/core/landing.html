What is Phishing?

Phishing is a social engineering attack that impersonates trusted people or brands to trick you into revealing secrets, clicking bad links, or sending money. It works on people — so the defense is people too.

{% include "includes/svg.html" with name="mail" %}

Email Phishing

Spoofed senders, lookalike domains, fake invoices, and malicious attachments.

{% include "includes/svg.html" with name="globe" %}

Fake Websites

Fraudulent login pages that copy real brands to harvest your password.

{% include "includes/svg.html" with name="phone" %}

Smishing & Vishing

Attacks by text message and phone, including MFA code scams.

{% include "includes/svg.html" with name="users" %}

Social Engineering

Manipulation tactics from gift-card fraud to pretexting and BEC.

{% include "includes/svg.html" with name="key" %}

Password & MFA

Build the habits that keep your accounts safe even when attackers come knocking.

{% include "includes/svg.html" with name="shield-alert" %}

Incident Response

Know exactly what to do when you spot — or click — a suspicious message.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a real phishing tool?

No. PhishAware is strictly defensive and educational. All scenarios use fictional organizations and domains. No real credentials, payments, or targets are ever involved.

Do the fake login pages collect my password?

Never. The simulated fields are decorative — whatever you type is discarded instantly and never stored or transmitted.

What does my training data include?

Only your account, module/lesson progress, quiz and scenario results, achievements, and any feedback you submit. No sensitive credentials.

How is the Security Awareness Score calculated?

It combines your training progress (40%), quiz average (35%), and scenario performance (25%).
